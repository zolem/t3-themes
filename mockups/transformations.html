T3 Code transformations

Five concepts that rebuild T3 in a game's own interface language instead of recoloring it. Each one keeps T3's layout (sidebar, chat, composer and the right panel, open here to a browser or a diff) but gives every part a new role in that world. Variants switch with the buttons above a mock.

Halo · Visor Iron Man · Workshop Diablo · Sanctuary Warcraft · Realm StarCraft · Command
Halo · Dark

Visor

You see T3 through a helmet visor. The app frame disappears and everything becomes a heads-up display floating on curved glass: the thread title is a waypoint on a compass, your threads are a fireteam with shield bars, and the browser becomes a locked-on target view.

Doable with CSS: the HUD layout, roster rows, shield bars colored by thread state, transmission styling, reticle send button, target frame. Decorative: the context fill level, tracker blip positions and the change counter, unless T3 exposes those values.

Header
Shield bar over a compass; the thread title is the waypoint, with other threads as markers
Sidebar
Fireteam roster: numbered callsigns with shield bars for state (engaged, awaiting orders, down, complete)
Chat
Incoming transmissions from an AI hologram; your messages are tagged Operator
Composer
"Open channel" panel with a targeting reticle as the send button
Right panel
Browser as a target view: corner brackets, lock-on reticle, "RANGE 3000" for localhost:3000
Corners
Ammo counter for lines changed, grenade pips for pending approvals, motion tracker for threads

Iron Man · Dark

Workshop

T3 becomes a holographic workbench. The three columns turn into glass panes floating in 3D over a glowing floor grid, angled toward you like projections around a workstation. Threads are suit bays with power rings, the assistant talks through an arc-reactor voice readout, and the browser is a hologram with callouts pinned to it.

Doable with CSS: the 3D-angled panes, floor grid, power rings colored by state, voice-style composer, hologram frame and scan line over the browser. Decorative: callouts on the page (CSS can't see inside the browser), gauge values and render stats. The angled browser pane needs a real-app check, since tilting it may affect clicking.

Whole app
Three floating glass panes in perspective over a holotable floor

Header
Thread title in HUD brackets between two arc gauges (context and usage)
Chat
Assistant speaks through an arc-reactor avatar with a live waveform
Composer
A rounded voice-command bar with a waveform and an arc-reactor send button
Right panel
Browser projected as a tilted hologram with callouts and a projector beam

Diablo · Dark

Sanctuary

T3 becomes an action-RPG screen. The composer moves into a stone skill bar flanked by a health orb and a mana orb. The sidebar becomes your stash with a tab per project and threads as loot named in rarity colors. Each thread opens like entering a new zone, and the diff becomes an item comparison: what's equipped on main versus the new drop.

Doable with CSS: the bottom HUD around the composer, project tabs, rarity colors by thread state, zone banner, drop cap, item-tooltip frames around the diff. Decorative: orb fill levels (context and usage), the progress bar, and the "+9 tests" line. Diff lines can be recolored but not restyled.

Sidebar
Stash: projects are tabs, threads are items. Legendary = running, Rare = needs you, Set = done, Broken = failed, Common = idle
Header
Zone banner: "Entering · Refactor Auth Middleware"
Chat
Assistant replies on a dark journal page with a drop cap; tool calls as italic narration
Composer
Stone skill bar: health orb (context), mana orb (usage), progress bar for the current run, skill slots for attach, model, access, plan and send ("Cast")
Right panel
Diff as an item comparison: "Currently equipped · main" over the new legendary, with an empty socket waiting for review

Realm

T3 becomes an MMO interface. You get a player frame for the model and a target frame for the current thread, with a cast bar showing what's running right now. The sidebar becomes a quest log with ! and ? markers and objectives. Tool calls read like a combat log, the composer is a chat box with channel tabs above an action bar, and the browser opens as the world map, with the right panel's tabs as buttons around a minimap.

Doable with CSS: frames, quest log rows and markers by state, objectives built from each thread's existing branch and time lines, combat-log prefix, chat tabs, action-bar buttons, map frame and the minimap tab buttons. Decorative: health, mana and experience values, cast-bar progress and the minimap art.

Composer

Command

T3 becomes an RTS command interface. The top bar shows resources (tokens, cost, running threads as supply) and the session clock. The sidebar is your unit selection: each thread is a wireframe unit colored by health, with control-group hotkeys per project. The composer becomes the bottom console, with the conversation minimap, an order line and a command card of hotkeyed buttons. The diff is a tactical readout with grid coordinates.

Doable with CSS: plates and console layout, wireframe units colored by thread state, selection ring, command-card buttons, the minimap (T3 already has a conversation minimap), tactical diff frame, and the race skins. Decorative: resource numbers, the unit icon art and "upgrade complete" line. Diff lines can be recolored but not restyled.
